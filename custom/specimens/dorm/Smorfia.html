The Astral Smorfia of the Ninth Moon

In the year 88421 of the Celestial Spiral, humanity no longer dreamed. Dreams had become a regulated resource harvested by the enormous machine-city known as Nebulora. Every citizen received exactly seven dreams per year, carefully distributed by the Ministry of Sleep. The authorities claimed that uncontrolled dreaming had once caused entire civilizations to vanish into their own imaginations. Vast towers processed billions of subconscious signals every hour and converted them into standardized dream sequences approved by the government. Children learned numerical dream theory before they learned history. Artists no longer painted visions from their minds but instead licensed dream packages from state archives. Most people accepted this system because no living citizen could remember a world where dreams had been free.

Yet beyond the Spiral existed a forbidden moon called Morphyria, where dreams were said to grow naturally like forests. Ancient legends claimed that every dream corresponded to a sacred number, and that these numbers formed a mysterious code known as the Astral Smorfia. The oldest star maps referred to the moon only through symbols rather than coordinates, as though its location changed according to the observer's thoughts. Entire fleets had searched for Morphyria throughout history, yet most returned empty-handed or reported contradictory findings. Some captains described oceans of crystal, while others spoke of floating mountains made from sleeping memories. The idea of symbolic numbers survived for thousands of years and inspired countless secret societies devoted to dream interpretation. Scholars argued endlessly about whether the numbers predicted reality or actively created it.

The Discovery

Captain Elian Voss commanded the exploration vessel Silent Comet. During a survey of Morphyria, his crew discovered crystalline trees whose leaves displayed glowing numbers. Whenever a leaf fell, a sleeping crew member experienced a vivid dream. The phenomenon occurred regardless of species, language, or cultural background. Security recordings revealed that the numbers changed color moments before a dream began. Some leaves dissolved into stardust after falling, while others continued glowing for weeks inside laboratory containers. The crew quickly abandoned their original mission objectives and dedicated all available resources to studying the mysterious forest. News of the discovery spread through encrypted channels and triggered immediate interest from the highest levels of Nebuloran society.

Scientist Lyra Sen recorded the first observations. To her astonishment, the same dream symbols repeatedly generated the same numbers. A silver cat always appeared with the number three. A radiant mother-like figure consistently appeared with the number eight. The crew began assembling a catalog they called the Original Astral Smorfia. Lyra filled thousands of pages with cross-references, sketches, and statistical analyses. Her findings suggested that the relationship between dream symbols and numbers was not random but part of an immense informational structure. Every new entry seemed connected to dozens of others through patterns too complex for ordinary computation.

The table resembled fragments of an ancient symbolic system in which specific numbers corresponded to recurring dream images. As the catalog expanded, the crew noticed that certain numbers frequently appeared together, forming clusters similar to constellations. These clusters generated related dream themes across different individuals. Historians aboard the expedition compared the findings with forgotten myths from hundreds of worlds. To their surprise, many civilizations had independently described identical dream symbols despite never having contact with one another. This suggested that the Astral Smorfia might predate interstellar civilization itself. Some researchers even proposed that it originated before the formation of the galaxy.

The Dream Ocean

Deep beneath Morphyria's surface lay the Dream Ocean, a liquid composed entirely of memories that had never happened. When the crew descended into its luminous waters, they witnessed impossible visions: cities floating upside down, whales made of stars, and clocks that counted backward toward creation. The ocean emitted soft harmonic sounds that resembled distant choirs singing in unknown languages. Instruments consistently failed whenever they attempted to measure its depth. Autonomous probes transmitted images of landscapes that appeared to change according to the observer's expectations. Some explorers reported meeting alternate versions of themselves living entirely different lives. Others encountered civilizations that existed only for a few seconds before fading into the surrounding liquid.

Each vision produced new numbers. Soon the crew realized that the numbers were coordinates, not merely symbols. They pointed toward hidden regions of the universe that ordinary navigation could never detect. By following these coordinates, the Silent Comet discovered corridors connecting distant parts of space through dream-generated shortcuts. Journeys that should have taken centuries required only minutes. Entire star systems previously believed to be fictional suddenly became accessible. The discoveries transformed astrophysics and challenged every accepted model of cosmic geography. The universe appeared far larger and stranger than anyone had imagined.

Further exploration revealed that each coordinate contained structures unlike anything previously observed. Some regions housed living nebulae capable of remembering conversations. Others contained geometric planets whose landscapes rearranged themselves according to mathematical principles. The coordinate associated with the Mad Astronomer held an enormous observatory orbiting a black hole made entirely of reflected memories. Crews stationed there reported dreams so vivid that they could continue them consciously while awake. Every new expedition returned with additional entries for the Astral Smorfia, expanding the catalog at an exponential rate. Entire academic disciplines emerged around the study of dream cartography.

The Number 90

At the center of every calculation appeared a forbidden value: 90. No dream described it directly. No machine could analyze it. Every equation collapsed whenever 90 appeared. Advanced quantum processors produced contradictory results when attempting to model it. Religious groups interpreted the anomaly as evidence of a divine intelligence hidden within mathematics. Government agencies classified all research related to the number and restricted access to authorized personnel. Despite these efforts, rumors spread rapidly across the Spiral. The mystery became one of the greatest obsessions in recorded history.

After years of research, Lyra concluded that 90 was not a destination. It was a living entity. An intelligence older than galaxies, existing inside the mathematics of dreams. According to her theory, all symbolic numbers were merely fragments of its awareness. The Astral Smorfia functioned as a language through which the entity communicated across time and space. Ancient myths, modern science, and unexplained cosmic phenomena suddenly appeared connected through a single framework. Many colleagues dismissed her conclusions as impossible. Yet every new experiment seemed to support her increasingly extraordinary claims.

The entity finally revealed itself during the convergence of seven moons. It spoke not in words but in sequences of symbols. The crew understood that reality itself had been generated from a dream dreamed by Number 90 long before time existed. Entire galaxies appeared around them as translucent patterns woven from numerical light. They witnessed stars being imagined into existence and black holes forming from forgotten thoughts. The entity showed them countless possible futures branching outward like luminous roots. Some futures ended in darkness, while others led to unimaginable flourishing. The choice, it explained, depended on whether intelligent beings continued to dream freely.

The Awakening

The citizens of Nebulora were invited to dream freely for the first time in millennia. The artificial dream distribution system was dismantled. People discovered creativity, imagination, and wonder. Streets once dominated by uniform architecture transformed into vibrant landscapes of artistic expression. Inventors created technologies inspired directly by their dreams. Musicians composed symphonies based on symbolic numerical sequences. Children began describing visions more complex than any educational simulation. Society experienced a cultural renaissance unlike anything in its history. Even the most skeptical observers acknowledged that something profound had changed.

As dreams returned, millions of new numbers appeared, expanding the Astral Smorfia beyond all known limits. The universe itself seemed to grow larger with every dream dreamt. Previously uncharted stars materialized along the edges of explored space. Entire dimensions became visible through dream-based observatories. The catalog that once fit inside a few research volumes expanded into planetary archives. Scholars estimated that only a tiny fraction of the system had been documented. Each generation discovered entirely new symbolic structures hidden within the expanding network of meanings.

Legend says that somewhere beyond the Spiral, the original Dream Tree still grows. Every leaf carries a number, every number hides a story, and every story is waiting for a dreamer brave enough to find it. Pilgrims travel across entire galaxies searching for traces of its roots. Some claim to have seen its branches stretching between stars like rivers of silver light. Others believe that every dream experienced by any living being is actually a leaf falling from the tree. No expedition has ever confirmed its location. Nevertheless, the legend endures because each generation continues discovering evidence that suggests the impossible may be true. And so the search for the Dream Tree continues across the endless horizons of the Astral Smorfia.
